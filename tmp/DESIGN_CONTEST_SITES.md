## Operating Guidelines

- For highly trend-sensitive web and digital design, prioritize results from the most recent one or two years whenever possible.
- Competition recruitment information loses value quickly after the submission period closes, so verify both the current year and the deadline during search.
- MCP response quality improves when award year, category, country, and source-site URL are extracted together from award websites.
- For practical Korean competitions, begin with hubs such as Design Jungle, Thinkcontest, and Wevity; for winners and reference work, use GDWEB together with global award websites.
